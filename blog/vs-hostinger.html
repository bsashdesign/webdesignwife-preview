Web Design Wife vs. Hostinger Website Builder: an honest comparison

By Ben Sash · 5 min read

The short answer: Hostinger's website builder is an affordable do-it-yourself option with AI tools to speed things up. It's a reasonable choice on a tight budget if you're comfortable doing the work.

What Hostinger Website Builder does well

  • Budget-friendly, with hosting included
  • AI tools to generate layouts and text
  • Simple drag-and-drop editing
  • Domain and email options in one place

Where it can fall short for a local business

  • Templates can feel basic
  • AI-generated content needs real editing to sound like you
  • Introductory prices often renew higher
  • Every update is still your job

Going with Hostinger Website Builder? Our tips

  1. Check the renewal price, not just the first-year price
  2. Edit AI text so it sounds like your business
  3. Add real photos and reviews
  4. Set up your Google Business Profile alongside the site
  5. Test your contact form after publishing
