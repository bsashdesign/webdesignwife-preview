Web Design Wife vs. Carrd: An Honest Comparison

By Ben Sash · 2 min read

The short answer: Carrd is one of the cheapest ways to get a simple one-page site, and it's great for what it is. The difference with my Essentials plan is that I design it, write it and keep it updated for you.

What Carrd Does Well

  • Very low cost
  • Quick to build a clean one-page site
  • Simple, focused editor
  • Good for landing pages and link-in-bio pages

Where It Can Fall Short for a Local Business

  • One page per site, with limited features
  • Design and writing are entirely up to you
  • No help with Google Maps, reviews or updates

Choose Carrd If…

…you're on a tight budget and happy to build a simple page yourself. That's a perfectly good reason, and plenty of local businesses do well with it.

Choose a Managed Website If…

…you want a professional one-page site, written and kept current for you.

What It Really Costs

It's one of the lowest-cost options available. The real limit is what a single page can do. With any do-it-yourself builder, the biggest cost is usually time. A few hours a month on updates, fixes and design tweaks adds up to several full days a year.

If You Want to Switch Later

A Carrd site is small, so rebuilding it elsewhere is quick if you outgrow it. Whatever you choose, make sure your domain name is registered in your own name. That's what lets you move without losing your web address.

Questions to Ask Yourself

  • Do I enjoy working on my website, or do I put it off?
  • When did I last update my hours, prices or photos?
  • Do I need to sell online, or do I mainly need customers to call, book or visit?
  • How much is an hour of my time worth to my business?

Going with Carrd? My Tips

  1. Lead with what you do and where you do it
  2. Add one clear button: call, book or get a quote
  3. Include a few real reviews
  4. Use your own domain name
  5. Keep the page short and scannable
