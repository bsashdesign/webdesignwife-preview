Web Design Wife vs. hiring a web design agency

By Ben Sash · 2 min read

The short answer: hire an agency for big, complex or highly custom projects with a budget to match. For a local business that needs a great informational site kept current, a managed site usually costs less and stays fresher.

What agencies do well

  • Deep brand strategy, research and custom design
  • Complex builds: large e-commerce stores, custom software, integrations
  • Teams of specialists for design, development, copy and marketing

Where agencies can fall short for small businesses

  • A large upfront bill, often several thousand dollars or more
  • Edits after launch are usually billed hourly or need a new quote
  • Because every change costs money, sites often go out of date
  • You may deal with account managers instead of the people doing the work

Choose an agency if…

…you need a large or highly custom project, like an online store with thousands of products or a web app. We take on custom projects like these too, quoted separately.

Choose a managed website if…

…you want a professional informational site for a predictable monthly price, with updates handled whenever you need them.

Questions to ask any agency

  1. How much do edits cost after launch?
  2. Who owns the domain name and the website?
  3. Who hosts the site, and who fixes it if it goes down?
  4. Will I talk directly to the people doing the work?
