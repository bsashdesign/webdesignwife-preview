Web Design Wife vs. Squarespace: an honest comparison

By Ben Sash · 2 min read

The short answer: Squarespace is one of the best options if you want a good-looking site and you're happy to build and update it yourself. If you want someone else to handle all of it, choose a managed site.

What Squarespace does well

  • Polished, designer-quality templates
  • A clean editor that's easy to learn
  • Built-in tools for scheduling, simple stores and email
  • Hosting, security and SSL included

Where it can fall short for a local business

  • Many sites use the same templates, so yours can look familiar
  • Layouts are less flexible once you move beyond the template
  • Every update is still yours to make
  • Templates only look great with great photos, and demo photos need replacing

Choose Squarespace if…

…you care about design, have good photos and enjoy tinkering. That's a perfectly good reason, and plenty of local businesses do well with it.

Choose a managed website if…

…you want a custom-feeling site and never want to log in to update it.

What it really costs

Plans are straightforward, but good photography is often the hidden cost. Templates look their best with professional-quality images. With any do-it-yourself builder, the biggest cost is usually time. A few hours a month on updates, fixes and design tweaks adds up to several full days a year.

If you want to switch later

Squarespace lets you export some content, like pages and blog posts, but the design itself stays behind. Moving usually means rebuilding the look elsewhere. Whatever you choose, make sure your domain name is registered in your own name. That's what lets you move without losing your web address.

Questions to ask yourself

  • Do I enjoy working on my website, or do I put it off?
  • When did I last update my hours, prices or photos?
  • Do I need to sell online, or do I mainly need customers to call, book or visit?
  • How much is an hour of my time worth to my business?

Going with Squarespace? Our tips

  1. Replace every demo photo and every line of placeholder text
  2. Put your phone number, hours and location on the homepage
  3. Fill in the SEO title and description for each page
  4. Keep navigation to five items or fewer
  5. Test your contact form by sending yourself a message
