Web Design Wife vs. Shopify: An Honest Comparison

By Ben Sash · 2 min read

The short answer: If your main goal is selling products online, use Shopify. It's excellent at what it does. My plans are for informational websites that bring in calls, bookings and visits, so I'm not the right fit for an online store.

What Shopify Does Well

  • Checkout, payments, inventory and shipping built in
  • Thousands of apps for reviews, subscriptions and marketing
  • A point-of-sale system if you also sell in person
  • Reliable hosting that handles busy sales days

Where It Can Fall Short for a Local Business

  • It's more than you need if you don't sell products online
  • Monthly fees, app subscriptions and transaction fees add up
  • You'll still manage products, photos and orders yourself

Choose Shopify If…

…you sell physical or digital products online. That's a perfectly good reason, and plenty of local businesses do well with it.

Choose a Managed Website If…

…you run a service business and want customers to call, book or visit.

What It Really Costs

Beyond the monthly plan, budget for payment processing fees, paid apps and a premium theme if you want one. With any do-it-yourself builder, the biggest cost is usually time. A few hours a month on updates, fixes and design tweaks adds up to several full days a year.

If You Want to Switch Later

Shopify lets you export products, customers and orders, which makes moving a store realistic. The theme and apps stay behind. Whatever you choose, make sure your domain name is registered in your own name. That's what lets you move without losing your web address.

Questions to Ask Yourself

  • Do I enjoy working on my website, or do I put it off?
  • When did I last update my hours, prices or photos?
  • Do I need to sell online, or do I mainly need customers to call, book or visit?
  • How much is an hour of my time worth to my business?

Going with Shopify? My Tips

  1. Start with a free theme and upgrade only if you need to
  2. Invest in clear product photos on a plain background
  3. Be upfront about shipping costs and delivery times
  4. Add real customer reviews to product pages
  5. Keep the number of apps small so your store stays fast
