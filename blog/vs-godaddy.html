Web Design Wife vs. GoDaddy Website Builder: An Honest Comparison

By Ben Sash, 2 min read

The short answer: GoDaddy's website builder is one of the quickest ways to get something online, especially if you already bought your domain there. It's basic by design, so it works best as a starting point.

What GoDaddy Website Builder Does Well

  • Very fast setup, with AI help writing a first draft
  • Domain, email and website in one place
  • Simple enough for anyone to use
  • Low cost to begin

Where It Can Fall Short for a Local Business

  • Limited design flexibility, so sites can look generic
  • AI-written text tends to sound like everyone else's
  • Frequent prompts to add paid extras
  • You're still responsible for every update

Choose GoDaddy Website Builder If…

…you need a simple site online today on a small budget. That's a perfectly good reason, and plenty of local businesses do well with it.

Choose a Managed Website If…

…you want a site that stands out from competitors and stays current.

What It Really Costs

Watch renewal prices and add-ons like email and extra security, which are often sold separately. With any do-it-yourself builder, the biggest cost is usually time. A few hours a month on updates, fixes and design tweaks comes to several full days a year.

If You Want to Switch Later

Sites made with the builder stay on GoDaddy. Your domain can be moved anywhere, but the site would need to be rebuilt. Whatever you choose, make sure your domain name is registered in your own name. That's what lets you move without losing your web address.

Questions to Ask Yourself

  • Do I enjoy working on my website, or do I put it off?
  • When did I last update my hours, prices or photos?
  • Do I need to sell online, or do I mainly need customers to call, book or visit?
  • How much is an hour of my time worth to my business?

Going with GoDaddy Website Builder? My Tips

  1. Rewrite the AI draft in your own words
  2. Swap stock images for real photos of your work
  3. Add your services, service area and reviews
  4. Double-check your contact details on every page
  5. Review your renewal prices before your first year ends
