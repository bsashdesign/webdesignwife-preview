Web Design Wife vs. Google Sites: An Honest Comparison

By Ben Sash · 2 min read

The short answer: Google Sites is free and easy if you have a Google account, but it's built mainly for internal and team pages. For a customer-facing business website, it's usually too limited.

What Google Sites Does Well

  • Free with a Google account
  • Very easy to use
  • Works well for internal pages, wikis and simple project sites

Where It Can Fall Short for a Local Business

  • Very limited design options
  • Few business features like reviews, booking or rich contact forms
  • Can look less professional to new customers

Choose Google Sites If…

…you need a free internal page or a temporary placeholder. That's a perfectly good reason, and plenty of local businesses do well with it.

Choose a Managed Website If…

…you want a professional site that turns visitors into customers.

What It Really Costs

It's free, but you'll likely want to upgrade to a proper website as your business grows. With any do-it-yourself builder, the biggest cost is usually time. A few hours a month on updates, fixes and design tweaks adds up to several full days a year.

If You Want to Switch Later

Content can be copied out easily because there's so little of it, but the design can't be moved. Whatever you choose, make sure your domain name is registered in your own name. That's what lets you move without losing your web address.

Questions to Ask Yourself

  • Do I enjoy working on my website, or do I put it off?
  • When did I last update my hours, prices or photos?
  • Do I need to sell online, or do I mainly need customers to call, book or visit?
  • How much is an hour of my time worth to my business?

Going with Google Sites? My Tips

  1. Use a clean theme and a clear headline
  2. Connect your own domain name
  3. Add a Google Form for simple inquiries
  4. Keep your contact details on every page
  5. Plan to upgrade once the business grows
