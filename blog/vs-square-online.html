Web Design Wife vs. Square Online: An Honest Comparison

By Ben Sash, 2 min read

The short answer: Square Online makes the most sense if you already run your business on Square, especially for online ordering or appointments. Many businesses combine a separate website with Square's ordering or booking pages.

What Square Online Does Well

  • Connects directly to your Square payments and items
  • Online ordering for pickup and delivery
  • Works with Square Appointments for bookings
  • Inexpensive to start

Where It Can Fall Short for a Local Business

  • Fewer design options than dedicated website builders
  • Built around selling, so storytelling and services pages are limited
  • Makes the most sense only if you already use Square

Choose Square Online If…

…you use Square and mainly need online ordering or booking. That's a perfectly good reason, and plenty of local businesses do well with it.

Choose a Managed Website If…

…you want a full website that tells your story. I can link it straight to your Square ordering or booking page.

What It Really Costs

The website is inexpensive, but payment processing fees are charged on every online order. With any do-it-yourself builder, the biggest cost is usually time. A few hours a month on updates, fixes and design tweaks comes to several full days a year.

If You Want to Switch Later

Your items and orders live in Square, so they stay with your Square account. The website design would need to be rebuilt elsewhere. Whatever you choose, make sure your domain name is registered in your own name. That's what lets you move without losing your web address.

Questions to Ask Yourself

  • Do I enjoy working on my website, or do I put it off?
  • When did I last update my hours, prices or photos?
  • Do I need to sell online, or do I mainly need customers to call, book or visit?
  • How much is an hour of my time worth to my business?

Going with Square Online? My Tips

  1. Keep your item photos and descriptions current
  2. Mark items sold out quickly so customers aren't disappointed
  3. Add your hours and pickup instructions clearly
  4. Link your Square site from your Google Business Profile
  5. Use the same business name everywhere online
