Web Design Wife vs. Wix: An Honest Comparison

By Ben Sash, 3 min read

The short answer: Wix is a great fit if you enjoy building things yourself and have the time to keep your site updated. If you'd rather never touch your website, a managed site is the better fit.

What Wix Does Well

  • A huge library of templates for almost every industry
  • A drag-and-drop editor that lets you put anything anywhere
  • An app market for bookings, online stores, forms and more
  • Hosting and security handled for you

Where It Can Fall Short for a Local Business

  • All that freedom makes it easy to end up with a cluttered page or a messy phone layout
  • You're the one making every update, from hours to holiday closures
  • Apps and upgrades can cost more than the base plan
  • Wix sites can't be exported, so moving away later usually means rebuilding

Choose Wix If…

…you like designing, have a few hours a month for updates and want full control. That's a perfectly good reason, and plenty of local businesses do well with it.

Choose a Managed Website If…

…you'd rather send a quick message and have changes done for you.

What It Really Costs

The base plan is only part of the picture. Many businesses add paid apps for bookings, reviews or forms, and the bigger cost is usually the hours you spend building and updating. With any do-it-yourself builder, the biggest cost is usually time. A few hours a month on updates, fixes and design tweaks comes to several full days a year.

If You Want to Switch Later

Wix sites can't be exported to another platform. If you leave, you keep your domain name and your content, but the design has to be rebuilt somewhere else. Whatever you choose, make sure your domain name is registered in your own name. That's what lets you move without losing your web address.

Questions to Ask Yourself

  • Do I enjoy working on my website, or do I put it off?
  • When did I last update my hours, prices or photos?
  • Do I need to sell online, or do I mainly need customers to call, book or visit?
  • How much is an hour of my time worth to my business?

Going with Wix? My Tips

  1. Start from a template made for your industry, then remove what you don't need
  2. Check every page in the mobile preview before you publish
  3. Connect your own domain name, registered in your name
  4. Add your hours, address and a clear “Call” or “Book” button near the top
  5. Link your Google Business Profile and ask customers for reviews
