The Service

Web Design Wife (Benjamin Sash, Brooklyn, NY) designs, hosts and maintains an informational website for your business for a monthly or yearly fee, as described on the pricing page.

Setup Fee and Billing

Every plan has a one-time setup fee, paid upfront when you sign up: $399 for Essentials, $899 for Business and $1,299 for Full Suite. Your subscription ($149, $229 or $299 a month, or $1,430, $2,150 or $2,870 a year with yearly billing) starts the day your site goes live, and your site only goes live after you approve it. Yearly billing discounts the subscription only, never the setup fee. By signing up you authorize both the setup fee and the recurring subscription, which begins at launch. Payments are processed by Stripe by card, digital wallet or US bank account (ACH).

In-Person Visits

Business and Full Suite include an in-person kickoff at your business and original photography of your business. We arrange the visit, its location and timing on our first call. Essentials is set up remotely, using photos you supply.

Applications (Business and Full Suite)

Business and Full Suite are by application. Applying is free and doesn't commit either of us. After you apply, we have a short video call. I may decline an application, for example if I can't serve the business well in person. If we're a good fit, I send you a private link to pay the setup fee and start. Essentials needs no application.

50% Launch Offer

The first five new clients I accept for the managed website service get 50% off the setup fee: $199 for Essentials, $449 for Business or $649 for Full Suite. The offer applies to the setup fee only; the monthly or yearly subscription is the regular plan price. The offer ends once five clients have signed up. If a client who received the launch offer is refunded under the 14-day design guarantee, the refund is the amount actually paid.

14-Day Design Guarantee

Your website will be designed and ready for your review within 14 days of our kickoff. If it isn't, you may cancel and get back everything you've paid. The 14 days pause while I'm waiting on something necessary from you, such as photos, access, information or feedback. The guarantee is about having the design ready for your review, not about when the site goes live: time you take to review it doesn't count against it.

Design Approval

Your site doesn't launch until you approve it. I'll keep refining the design within the agreed scope until you're happy to approve it. This covers refining the initial design, not starting over with a different scope or adding features beyond your plan. Once your site is live, payments are non-refundable, but you can cancel anytime.

Minor Edits

Every plan includes unlimited minor edits: small updates to existing content that take under 30 minutes each, done within one business day, like text, prices, hours, photos, staff, services or a promo banner. Redesigns, new sections, new features and rebuilt pages are a new project with its own fee, quoted upfront.

Ownership

Your domain name is registered in your name and belongs to you. Your content, including your text, photos and logo, belongs to you. The website's design and code are owned by Web Design Wife and provided as part of your plan until ownership transfers to you.

Owning Your Website

On every plan, monthly or yearly, after six years of continuous subscription, ownership of your custom website transfers to you. You may keep your subscription for hosting, updates and support, or move the site elsewhere. Before then, you may buy your website early for a set number of months of your current plan price: 24 during year one, 20 during year two, 16 during year three, 12 during year four, 8 during year five and 4 during year six.

Transfer Fee

Moving your website away from Web Design Wife, whether after an early buyout or after six years, has a one-time transfer fee of $299. Your ownership terms and transfer fee are locked in when you sign up; any later change applies to new clients only. A transfer includes an export of your site's code and content, or a handover to your new developer. AI assistants, hosted forms, analytics services, third-party feeds and other managed integrations may depend on Web Design Wife or third-party accounts and are not automatically included in a transfer.

Design Refresh (Business and Full Suite)

Business includes one design refresh every 4 years and Full Suite one every 3 years, on monthly or yearly billing. A refresh is optional and used when the client chooses, once it becomes available: we review the site together and I update its visual direction, such as imagery, typography, colors and styling, key sections and calls to action. A refresh works within the existing site and can include reworking pages and adding new ones, up to the page limit of your plan; a complete redesign or new features are a separate project, quoted upfront.

Case Studies

With your separate permission, and only if you'd like to, I may document parts of your project (such as the visit, photos and the design process) as a case study or for social media. It's never a condition of becoming a client, and you can say no or change your mind.

Cancelling

You can cancel anytime. Your plan runs to the end of the period you've paid for. When it ends, the website goes offline unless you buy it out. You keep your domain, and I'll send you your content.

Google Rankings

I work to set up your site and Google Maps profile to be found, but no one can guarantee a specific search ranking.
