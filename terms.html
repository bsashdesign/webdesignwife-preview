The Service

Web Design Wife (Benjamin Sash, Brooklyn, NY) designs, hosts and maintains an informational website for your business for a monthly or yearly fee, as described on the pricing page.

Setup Fee and Billing

Every package has a one-time setup fee, paid through the link I send you after our call: $399 for Essentials, $899 for Business and $1,299 for Full Suite. Your subscription ($149, $229 or $299 a month, or $1,430, $2,150 or $2,870 a year with yearly billing) starts the day your site goes live, and your site only goes live after you approve it. Yearly billing discounts the subscription only, never the setup fee. When you pay the setup fee, you authorize both the setup fee and the recurring subscription, which begins at launch. Payments are processed by Stripe by card, digital wallet or US bank account (ACH).

How We Work Together

Every package starts with a short phone call. If we agree on a package, I send you a link where you pay the setup fee and send me your business details. Everything after that, from your design review to every change after launch, happens by phone, email and text.

50% Launch Offer

The first five businesses to start the managed website service get 50% off the setup fee: $199 for Essentials, $449 for Business or $649 for Full Suite. The offer applies to the setup fee only; the monthly or yearly subscription is the regular package price. The offer ends once five businesses have started. If a client who received the launch offer is refunded under the 14-day design guarantee, the refund is the amount actually paid.

14-Day Design Guarantee

Your website will be designed and ready for your review within 14 days of our kickoff. If it isn't, you may cancel and get back everything you've paid. The 14 days pause while I'm waiting on something necessary from you, such as photos, access, information or feedback. The guarantee is about having the design ready for your review, not about when the site goes live: time you take to review it doesn't count against it.

Design Approval

Your site doesn't launch until you approve it. I'll keep refining the design within the agreed scope until you're happy to approve it. This covers refining the initial design, not starting over with a different scope or adding features beyond your package. Once your site is live, payments are non-refundable, but you can cancel anytime.

Everyday Changes

Every package includes your everyday changes: updates to what is already on your site, such as words, prices, hours, holiday notices, photos, staff, a new service on your services page or a new review featured, with the matching updates to your Google profile, live by the next business day. New pages beyond your package, new capabilities, photography, new page copy written from scratch, logo and print work, migrations and custom layouts are quoted upfront, and nothing is added to your bill without your agreement.

Ownership

Your domain name is registered in your name and belongs to you. Your content, including your text, photos and logo, belongs to you. The website's design and code are owned by Web Design Wife and provided as part of your package until ownership transfers to you.

Owning Your Website

On every package, monthly or yearly, after six years of continuous subscription, ownership of your website transfers to you. You may keep your subscription for hosting, updates and support, or move the site elsewhere. Before then, you may buy your website early for a set number of months of your current package price: 24 during year one, 20 during year two, 16 during year three, 12 during year four, 8 during year five and 4 during year six.

Transfer Fee

Moving your website away from Web Design Wife, whether after an early buyout or after six years, has a one-time transfer fee of $299. Your ownership terms and transfer fee are locked in when you pay the setup fee; any later change applies to new clients only. A transfer includes an export of your site's code and content, or a handover to your new developer. AI assistants, hosted forms, analytics services, third-party feeds and other managed integrations may depend on Web Design Wife or third-party accounts and are not automatically included in a transfer.

Design Refresh

Essentials and Business include one design refresh every 4 years and Full Suite one every 3 years, on monthly or yearly billing. A refresh is optional and used when the client chooses, once it becomes available: we review the site together and I update its visual direction, such as imagery, typography, colors and styling, key sections and calls to action. A refresh works within the existing site and can include reworking pages and adding new ones, within the pages of your package; a complete redesign or new features are a separate project, quoted upfront.

Case Studies

With your separate permission, and only if you'd like to, I may document parts of your project (such as screenshots and the design process) as a case study or for social media. It's never a condition of becoming a client, and you can say no or change your mind.

Cancelling

You can cancel anytime. Your package runs to the end of the period you've paid for. When it ends, the website goes offline unless you've bought it out or reached six years, when it's already yours. You keep your domain, and I'll send you your content.

Google Rankings

I work to set up your site and Google profile to be found, but no one can guarantee a specific search ranking.
