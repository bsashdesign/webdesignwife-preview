The Service

We design, host and maintain an informational website for your business for a monthly or yearly fee, as described on our pricing page.

Setup Fee

Monthly plans include a one-time setup fee, which is non-refundable. Yearly plans include setup at no charge.

Minor Edits

Every plan includes unlimited minor edits: small updates to existing content that take under 30 minutes each, like text, prices, hours, photos, staff, services or a promo banner. Redesigns, new sections, new features and rebuilt pages are a new project with its own fee, quoted upfront.

Ownership

Your domain name is registered in your name and belongs to you. Your content, including your text, photos and logo, belongs to you. The website's design and code are owned by Web Design Wife and provided as part of your plan. You may buy your website outright at any time; ask us for your current buyout price.

Cancelling

You can cancel as described in your plan. When your plan ends, the website goes offline unless you buy it out. You keep your domain, and we'll send you your content.

We work to set up your site and Google Maps profile to be found, but no one can guarantee a specific search ranking.
