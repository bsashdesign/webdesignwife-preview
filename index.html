FAQ

Good questions

Something else on your mind? Let's talk and I'll answer it personally.

Who owns the website?

There are two parts. Your web address (the domain name, like yourbusiness.com) is always registered in your name, and it's yours to keep. The website itself, meaning the design and code, is owned and maintained by us. That's what lets us keep it fast, secure and up to date for a monthly price instead of a big upfront bill. Your text, photos and logo are always yours too.

Can I buy my website outright?

Yes, at any time. The buyout price depends on your plan and drops the longer you've been a client. Ask us for your current price.

What if I want to cancel?

No hard feelings. You keep your web address, and we'll send you all your text and photos. The website goes offline when your plan ends, unless you buy it out.

What counts as a minor edit?

Small updates to existing content that take under 30 minutes each: text, prices, hours, photos, staff, services or a promo banner. New features, new sections or redesigning pages count as a new project.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Move up whenever you need more pages or features. Moving to a bigger plan may include a small setup fee for the new pages.

Do I have to write blog posts on Full Suite?

No. Each month you answer a short questionnaire, by typing or sending a voice memo. We turn your answers into a post, and nothing goes live until you approve it. Skip a month whenever you're busy.

Do you only work with New York businesses?

We focus on New York City first, because knowing the neighborhoods helps your site show up in local searches. We're happy to talk if you're outside the city, too.

Can you get me to the top of Google?

No one can honestly promise a ranking. What we can promise is that your site and Google Maps profile will be set up the right way to be found, and we'll keep them that way.

I need an online store or booking system. Can you help?

These plans are for informational websites. We can link to booking tools you already use, like Square, Vagaro or Calendly. For e-commerce and custom features, we take on custom projects. Get in touch and let's talk it through.
