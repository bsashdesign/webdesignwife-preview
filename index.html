        FAQ

        Good Questions

        Something else on your mind? Request a callback and I'll answer it personally.

        What if I don't like the design?

        You'll see your design before anything goes live, and nothing goes live until you approve it. If you don't love it and decide not to move forward, you get back 100% of what you paid, setup fee included.

        Can I cancel?

        Yes, anytime. You keep your web address, and I'll send you all your text and photos. The website goes offline when your plan ends, unless you buy it early or you've been with me six years, in which case it's already yours.

        Who owns the site?

        Your web address (like yourbusiness.com) is always registered in your name. During your subscription, until you buy it early or ownership transfers after six years, the website design and code are owned and maintained by Web Design Wife. Your text, photos and logo are always yours.
