FAQ

Good Questions

Something else on your mind? Let's talk and I'll answer it personally.

Who owns the website?

There are two parts. Your web address (the domain name, like yourbusiness.com) is always registered in your name, and it's yours to keep. The website itself, meaning the design and code, is owned and maintained by me. That's what lets me keep it fast, secure and up to date for a monthly price instead of a big upfront bill. Your text, photos and logo are always yours too.

What if I don't like the design?

You'll see your design before anything goes live. If you don't love it and decide not to move forward, you get back 100% of what you paid, setup fee included. Once you approve it and the site goes live, payments are non-refundable, but you can cancel anytime.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel anytime. Change plans as your needs change. Buy the site early if you want to leave with it. Your buyout gets cheaper every year. After six years, you own it. If you do cancel, no hard feelings: you keep your web address, and I'll send you all your text and photos. The website goes offline when your plan ends, unless you buy it early or you've been with me six years, in which case it's already yours.

When do I own my website?

After six years with me, ownership of your custom website transfers to you. Keep your subscription for hosting, updates and support, or take your site with you for a simple transfer fee.

Want to leave with it sooner? You can buy it early, and the price drops every year:

  • During year 124 months of your plan
  • During year 220 months
  • During year 316 months
  • During year 412 months
  • During year 58 months
  • During year 64 months
  • After 6 yearsIt's yours

This applies to every plan, monthly or yearly. Any transfer, early or after six years, has a one-time $299 transfer fee, and that fee is locked in when you sign up: if it goes up later, it only changes for new clients. I hand the site over to you or your new developer, including a full export of the code and content.

AI assistants, hosted forms, analytics, third-party feeds and other managed integrations may depend on Web Design Wife or third-party accounts, so they aren't automatically included in a transfer. I'll tell you exactly what moves and what you'd need to set up again.

What counts as a minor edit?

Small updates to existing content that take under 30 minutes each: text, prices, hours, photos, staff, services or a promo banner. New features, new sections or redesigning pages count as a new project.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Move up whenever you need more pages or features. Moving to a bigger plan may include a small setup fee for the new pages.

Do I have to write blog posts on Full Suite?

No. Each month you answer a short questionnaire, by typing or sending a voice memo. I turn your answers into a post, and nothing goes live until you approve it. Skip a month whenever you're busy.

What's the annual design refresh on Full Suite?

So your site never looks four years old, whether you pay monthly or yearly. Once a year we review it together and I refresh its look and content: updated sections, new photos, styling tweaks and sharper calls to action. It's a refresh, not a from-scratch redesign, but it keeps the site current year after year.

Can you get me to the top of Google?

No one can honestly promise a ranking. What I can promise is that your site and Google Maps profile will be set up the right way to be found, and I'll keep them that way.

Need something different, like a custom package, an online store or a booking system?

These plans are for informational websites. I can link to booking tools you already use, like Square, Vagaro or Calendly. For e-commerce, custom features or a package that fits your business better, I take on custom projects. Ask about a custom package and let's talk it through.
