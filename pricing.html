Cancel, Switch or Own It

Cancel Anytime

No contracts. If you cancel, your site stays live to the end of the month or year you've paid for. You keep your web address and I send you all your text and photos. After that the site goes offline, unless you've bought it or been with me six years.

Switch Packages

Move up or down as your business changes. If moving down means trimming pages or features, I tell you any one-time cost before anything changes.

Own Your Site After Six Years

Stay six years and the site is yours. Want it sooner? Buy it out; the price drops every year.

After six years the buyout is $0: the site is yours. If you move it away from Web Design Wife, the $299 technical transfer fee covers packaging the site and handing the code and content to you or your new developer.

Good Questions

Something else on your mind? Schedule a Call and ask me directly.

What Happens on the Call?

15 minutes by phone. You tell me about your business, I tell you which package fits and what it costs, and you decide in your own time.

Why a Call Instead of Paying Online?

So you're never guessing. I confirm the package fits before you pay anything, and you hear the price from me.

Do We Need to Meet?

No. Everything happens by phone, email and text, from our first call to every change after launch.

When Does My Subscription Start?

The day your site goes live, and it only goes live once you approve it. Setup is paid to begin, your design is ready within 14 days, and we refine it until you approve it.

What Counts as an Everyday Change?

Updates to what's already on your site: words, prices, hours, photos, staff, a new service or a holiday notice, with the Google profile updates that go with them. New pages or features are quoted first.

What If I Don't Like the Design?

We keep refining it, within what we agreed, until you're happy to approve it, and nothing launches or bills monthly before then. If I miss the 14 days, you can cancel and get back everything you paid. Once the site is live, payments are non-refundable, but you can cancel anytime.

Who Provides the Photos?

You do, from your phone is fine. On Business I choose and prepare the best ones; on Full Suite I can arrange a photographer, quoted separately.

How Much of the Design Is Personalized?

Every site is built on my house system, personalized: your logo, colors, type, photos and words, never a template with a logo dropped in. Every site gets one of three type pairings; Business adds a signature piece on your Home page, and Full Suite adds three custom sections.

How Does the Design Refresh Work?

Every 3 or 4 years, depending on your package, we review the site together and I refresh the imagery, typography, colors and key sections. It's the same site looking current again, not a rebuild.

Who Owns the Website?

Your web address is always registered in your name, and your text, photos and logo are always yours. The design and code belong to Web Design Wife until you buy the site out or reach six years, when it becomes yours.

What Is the 50% Launch Offer?

The service is new, so the first five businesses that sign up get 50% off setup: $199, $449 or $649 instead of $399, $899 or $1,299. Your monthly price is the regular price.

Can You Get Me to the Top of Google?

No one can honestly promise a ranking. I can promise your site and Google profile are set up the right way to be found, and I keep them that way.

Need Something Different, Like an Online Store or a Booking System?

These packages are for websites that bring in calls and visits. I can link to the booking tool you already use; for an online store or custom features, I quote a custom project. Ask About a Custom Project
