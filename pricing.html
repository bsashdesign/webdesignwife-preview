No lock-in

Cancel, Switch or Own It

Cancel anytime

No contracts. If you cancel, no hard feelings: you keep your web address, and I'll send you all your text and photos. The website goes offline when your plan ends, unless you buy it early or you've been with me six years, in which case it's already yours.

Switch plans

You can move up or down as your needs change. If moving to a smaller plan means consolidating pages or removing features to fit that plan, I'll explain any one-time reconfiguration cost before anything changes. Your lower rate begins once the site has been converted to the lower plan.

Own your site after six years

Stay six years and the site is yours. Want it sooner? Buy it out; the price drops every year.

Plus a one-time $299 transfer fee, locked in when you sign up. I hand the site over to you or your new developer, with a full export of the code and content.

AI assistants, hosted forms, analytics, third-party feeds and other managed integrations may depend on Web Design Wife or third-party accounts, so they aren't automatically included in a transfer. I'll tell you exactly what moves and what you'd need to set up again.

FAQ

Good Questions

Something else on your mind? Request a callback and I'll answer it personally.

Who owns the website?

There are two parts. Your web address (the domain name, like yourbusiness.com) is always registered in your name, and it's yours to keep. During your subscription, until you buy it early or ownership transfers after six years, the website design and code are owned and maintained by Web Design Wife. That's what lets me keep it fast, secure and up to date for a monthly price instead of a big upfront bill. Your text, photos and logo are always yours.

When does my subscription start?

The day your site goes live, and it only goes live once you've approved it. Until then, the only charge is the setup fee you paid when you signed up.

What is the Founding 5?

The managed website service is new, so I'm opening it to five founding businesses first. Their setup fee is $1 instead of the usual $199 to $599. The monthly or yearly plan price is the normal price. When all five spots are taken, the offer ends.

What if I don't like the design?

You'll see your design before anything goes live. If you don't love it and decide not to move forward, you get back 100% of what you paid, setup fee included. Once you approve it and the site goes live, payments are non-refundable, but you can cancel anytime.

Do I have to write blog posts on Full Suite?

No. Each month you answer a short questionnaire, by typing or sending a voice memo. I turn your answers into a post, and nothing goes live until you approve it. Skip a month whenever you're busy.

How does the design refresh work?

Business includes a design refresh every 3 years, and Full Suite every 2 years. You choose when to use it: once it's available, we review your site together and I refresh the imagery, typography, colors and styling, key sections and calls to action. I can rework your pages and add new ones, up to your plan's page limit. It's the same site looking current again, not a rebuild. On Essentials, a refresh is available separately.

Can you get me to the top of Google?

No one can honestly promise a ranking. What I can promise is that your site and Google Maps profile will be set up the right way to be found, and I'll keep them that way.

Need something different, like a custom package, an online store or a booking system?

These plans are for informational websites. I can link to booking tools you already use, like Square, Vagaro or Calendly. For e-commerce, custom features or a package that fits your business better, I take on custom projects. Ask about a custom package and let's talk it through.
