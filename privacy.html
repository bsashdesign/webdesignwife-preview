Who I Am

Web Design Wife is run by Benjamin Sash in Brooklyn, NY. Questions about your information go to ben@webdesignwife.com.

What I Collect

When you fill out a form on this site, I collect what you give me: your name, email, phone number, business name and address, website and Google Maps links, and your message. If you subscribe to my emails, I keep your email address. My host may also keep standard server logs, like IP addresses and pages visited.

How I Use It

To reply to you, prepare the audit or call you asked for, provide my services if you become a client, and, if you subscribed, send you tips, news and deals by email (about once a month, and every email has an unsubscribe link). I don't sell your information. I only share it with the tools I use to run the business: payments are processed by Stripe (I never see or store your full card number), calls are scheduled through Cal.com, and email and forms go through their providers.

How Long I Keep It

I keep inquiries for as long as they're useful for following up, and client records for as long as we work together and as required for tax and legal purposes. If you unsubscribe, I stop emailing you right away.

Your Choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you at any time. Just email me or use the contact page.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
