Who I Am

Web Design Wife is run by Benjamin Sash in Brooklyn, NY. Questions about your information go to ben@webdesignwife.com.

What I Collect

This site collects information in three places, and only what you type in:

  • The free website audit: your website or Google Maps link, your name and your email.
  • The note form on the contact page: your name, your email and your note.
  • Booking a call: your name, email, phone number and the time you pick, through Cal.com.

If you become a client, I also keep the business details you send me and a record of your payments. My host may keep standard server logs, like IP addresses and pages visited. There are no email subscriptions on this site.

How I Use It

To reply to you, prepare the audit or call you asked for, and provide my services if you become a client. I don't sell your information. I only share it with the tools I use to run the business: payments are processed by Stripe (I never see or store your full card number), calls are scheduled through Cal.com, and email and forms go through their providers.

How Long I Keep It

I keep inquiries for as long as they're useful for following up, and client records for as long as we work together and as required for tax and legal purposes.

Your Choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you at any time. Just email me or use the contact page.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top of this page.
